## Getting Started

### No Account Required

You can browse and shop on our website **without creating an account**. You only need to provide basic information at checkout.

### Creating an Account (Optional)

To save your preferences and track orders more easily:

1. Click "Sign Up" in the top right
2. Enter:
   - Email address
   - Password (min 8 characters)
   - First & last name
   - Phone number (optional)
3. Click "Create Account"
4. Verify your email (link sent to inbox)
5. Done! You're ready to shop

### Logging In

1. Click "Login" in the top right
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Login"
4. You're logged in!

### Forgot Password

1. Click "Login"
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your email
4. Click "Send Reset Link"
5. Check your email for password reset link
6. Click link and create new password
7. Login with new password

## Checkout Process

### Step 1: Select Quantity & Customizations

1. On product page, select quantity (number of copies)
2. Choose customizations:
   - **Theme/Color**: Select from available options
   - **Size**: Choose paper size or format
   - **Variations**: Any product-specific options
3. Click "Add to Cart"

### Step 2: Review Cart

1. Click cart icon (top right)
2. Review items:
   - Product name
   - Quantity
   - Selected theme/color
   - Price per item
3. View subtotal
4. To modify:
   - Change quantity: Click +/- buttons
   - Change customizations: Click item to edit
   - Remove item: Click trash icon
5. Click "Proceed to Checkout"

### Step 3: Shipping Address

1. Enter or select address:
   - Full name
   - Phone number
   - City
   - Postal code
   - Street address

2. Select delivery option:
   - **Standard**: 3-5 business days
   - **Express**: 1-2 business days
   - **Same Day**: If available in your area

3. Shipping fee will be calculated
4. Click "Continue"

### Step 4: Review & Confirm

Before proceeding to payment:
1. Review order summary:
   - Items and customizations
   - Shipping address
   - Subtotal
   - Tax (if applicable)
   - Shipping fee
   - **Total amount**

2. Edit if needed (back button)
3. Click "Proceed to Payment"

### Step 5: Select Payment Method

See next section for details on each method.

### Step 6: Payment Confirmation

After payment:
1. See confirmation page with:
   - Order number
   - Receipt
   - Download/print options
2. You'll receive confirmation email
3. Receive SMS with order tracking link (optional)
4. Click link to track your order

## Payment Methods

### 1. Card Payment (Visa, Mastercard, etc.)

**How it works:**
- Powered by Paymob
- Safe, encrypted payment
- Instantly confirmed

**Process:**
1. At checkout, select "Card Payment"
2. Click "Pay Now"
3. You'll be redirected to payment page
4. Enter card details:
   - Card number (on front)
   - Expiry date (MM/YY)
   - CVC (3 digits on back)
   - Cardholder name
5. Click "Pay"
6. Payment is processed
7. Return to order confirmation

**Cards accepted:**
- Visa
- Mastercard
- Egyptian Payment Cards (all types)

**Security:**
- All payments are PCI-DSS compliant
- Your card details are never stored
- Encrypted transmission

### 2. Mobile Wallet - Vodafone Cash

**How it works:**
- Deduct from your Vodafone Cash balance
- Instant payment
- Works on any Vodafone phone

**Process:**
1. At checkout, select "Vodafone Cash"
2. Enter your Vodafone phone number
3. Click "Pay with Vodafone"
4. You'll receive USSD prompt or SMS
5. Reply with your PIN
6. Payment is confirmed
7. Return to order confirmation

**Requirements:**
- Active Vodafone number
- Vodafone Cash account with balance
- Vodafone Cash PIN

### 3. Mobile Wallet - InstaPay

**How it works:**
- Deduct from your InstaPay balance
- Instant payment
- Works with any bank

**Process:**
1. At checkout, select "InstaPay"
2. Enter your phone number (registered with bank)
3. Click "Pay with InstaPay"
4. You'll receive app notification
5. Open InstaPay app and approve
6. Payment is confirmed
7. Return to order confirmation

**Requirements:**
- Bank account registered with InstaPay
- InstaPay app installed
- Sufficient balance

### 4. WhatsApp Payment

**How it works:**
- Send payment proof via WhatsApp
- Admin verifies and confirms
- No instant confirmation (typically within 1 hour)

**Process:**
1. At checkout, select "WhatsApp Payment"
2. Your order is placed with "pending" status
3. You'll see payment details:
   - Amount to send
   - Store WhatsApp number
   - What to send as proof
4. Open WhatsApp and message the number
5. Send:
   - "Paying for order [ORDER_NUMBER]"
   - Screenshot of payment/transfer
   - Your name and email
6. Wait for confirmation (usually 30 mins - 1 hour)
7. You'll receive notification when payment is confirmed
8. Order processing begins

**Best for:**
- Bank transfers
- Local payment apps not listed above
- If other methods don't work

**Tips:**
- Send payment proof clearly
- Include order number in message
- Check your inbox for payment instructions
- Be available for questions if needed

### Payment Comparison

| Method | Speed | Fee | Requirements |
|--------|-------|-----|--------------|
| Card | Instant | None | Visa/Mastercard |
| Vodafone Cash | Instant | None | Vodafone account |
| InstaPay | Instant | None | Bank account |
| WhatsApp | 30 mins-1 hour | None | WhatsApp/Bank account |

### Save Payment Method

If logged in, you can save your payment method:
1. During checkout, check "Save for future purchases"
2. Next time, select saved method
3. Payment is faster

### Failed Payment

If your payment fails:
1. You'll see error message explaining why
2. Check:
   - Card details are correct
   - Card is not expired
   - Sufficient funds
   - Card is not blocked
3. Try again with correct details
4. Contact your bank if problem persists
5. Try alternative payment method

## Tracking Orders

### After Purchase

**You'll receive:**
1. Order confirmation email
2. SMS with tracking link (optional)
3. Access to order in your account

### View Order Status

**Without Account:**
1. Click tracking link in email/SMS
2. View order status page
3. See order timeline

**With Account:**
1. Login to your account
2. Click "My Orders"
3. See all your orders
4. Click order to see details

### Order Status Meanings

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Pending** | Payment received, preparing for delivery |
| **Processing** | Items being prepared/packaged |
| **Picked Up** | Delivery driver has collected package |
| **In Transit** | On the way to your location |
| **Delivered** | Successfully delivered to you |
| **Failed** | Delivery attempt failed, will retry |
| **Returned** | Returned to store (refund initiated) |

### Tracking Details

When you view order status, you'll see:
- **Current Status**: Where your package is
- **Timeline**: History of all status changes
- **Tracking Number**: Official tracking reference
- **Delivery Personnel**: Name and phone (if available)
- **Location**: Current location (if GPS available)
- **Estimated Delivery**: Expected delivery date/time
- **Delivery Notes**: Any special notes from driver

### Receive Notifications

Updates are sent via:
- **Email**: Status change confirmations
- **SMS**: Quick status updates (if opted in)
- **In-App**: Notifications visible in your account

To control notifications:
1. Login to account
2. Click Settings → Notifications
3. Choose notification methods:
   - Email notifications ☑
   - SMS notifications ☑
   - Order updates ☑
   - Promotional emails ☐

### Contact During Delivery

If tracking shows delivery person nearby:
- Click "Contact Driver" button
- Send message via SMS or call
- Driver will respond via text
- Provide delivery instructions if needed

## Returns & Refunds

### Return Policy

- **Digital Products**: Generally non-refundable once downloaded
- **Customizable Products**: May have limited customization changes
- **Check Your Seller**: Each seller sets their own return policy

View return policy:
1. Go to product page
2. Scroll to "Return Policy" section
3. Read full terms

### Request Return

If product qualifies:
1. Login to account
2. Go to "My Orders"
3. Find order
4. Click "Request Return"
5. Select reason:
   - Product is defective
   - Not as described
   - Other reason
6. Add comments with details
7. Upload photos if applicable
8. Click "Submit Return Request"

### Return Process

1. **Admin Reviews** (1-2 business days): Admin checks your request
2. **Return Approved**: You'll receive return instructions and shipping label
3. **Ship Product**: Package item and drop at indicated location
4. **In Transit**: We receive and inspect product
5. **Refund Processed**: Money refunded to original payment method
6. **Confirmation**: You'll receive refund notification

**Timeline**: 7-10 business days total

### Refund Status

Track refund progress:
1. Go to order details
2. Scroll to "Return Status" section
3. See current step in refund process
4. Click "Return Details" for more info

### Refund Methods

Refunds go to:
- **Original payment method**: Default (card, Vodafone, InstaPay)
- **Store Credit**: Offered for faster processing
- **Alternative**: Request different method (WhatsApp/bank transfer)

Request refund method change:
1. On return page, click "Change Refund Method"
2. Select new method
3. Provide details if needed
4. Click "Confirm"

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: No! You can shop without an account. Creating one is optional and saves your preferences.

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
A: Standard delivery is 3-5 business days. Express is 1-2 days. Check at checkout for your area.

**Q: Can I change my order after placing it?**
A: If not yet processed, contact us quickly. We'll try to modify it. After processing, contact the seller.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
A: Currently we ship within Egypt. We're expanding soon!

### Payment Questions

**Q: Are my card details safe?**
A: Yes! We use Paymob, a PCI-DSS certified payment processor. Your card never touches our servers.

**Q: Can I save my payment method?**
A: Yes, if you create an account. Check "Save for future purchases" at checkout.

**Q: What if my payment fails?**
A: Check card is not expired, has sufficient funds, and isn't blocked. Try another method. Contact your bank if issue persists.

**Q: Do you charge fees for payments?**
A: No additional fees. The price shown is what you pay.

**Q: Can I pay in installments?**
A: Currently not available. We may add this soon.

### Delivery Questions

**Q: Can I track my order in real-time?**
A: Yes! Click the tracking link in your email. You'll see location, status, and delivery person details.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address?**
A: If not yet picked up, contact us quickly. We'll try to change it.

**Q: What if nobody is home for delivery?**
A: Driver will leave note. You can reschedule or provide alternative instructions in tracking page.

**Q: Can I have multiple items delivered separately?**
A: They're typically delivered together. Contact seller if you need separate deliveries.

**Q: How do I contact the delivery driver?**
A: Click "Contact Driver" on tracking page. Send message via SMS/WhatsApp.

### Return Questions

**Q: How long do I have to return?**
A: Depends on seller's policy (usually 14-30 days). Check product page for details.

**Q: Do I pay for return shipping?**
A: Seller provides return label. You ship at no cost.

**Q: When will I get my refund?**
A: 7-10 business days after we receive returned item and inspect it.

**Q: Can I exchange instead of refund?**
A: Contact seller to discuss exchange options. Not all products qualify.

### Account Questions

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page. You'll receive reset link via email.

**Q: Can I change my email?**
A: Yes. Go to Settings → Profile → Change Email. You'll need to verify new email.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your data will be removed after 30 days.

**Q: What happens to my orders if I delete my account?**
A: Orders remain in the system. You can still track them using email/phone.
