## Getting Started

### Your First Day

1. **Receive Login Credentials**
   - You'll get email with login link
   - Click link to set password
   - Save credentials securely

2. **Access the Delivery App**
   - Go to https://yourdomain.com/delivery
   - Or download mobile app from App Store
   - Login with your credentials

3. **Complete Your Profile**
   - Update personal information
   - Add phone number
   - Verify email
   - Upload photo (optional but helpful)

4. **Set Your Availability**
   - Indicate your working hours
   - Select days available
   - Set service area
   - Mark as "available" to accept orders

### Your Dashboard

When you login, you'll see:
- **Pending Orders**: Awaiting assignment
- **Assigned Orders**: Your current deliveries
- **Completed**: Today's finished deliveries
- **Stats**: Your performance metrics

## Accessing Your Orders

### Receive Order Assignment

**Automatic Assignment:**
1. System assigns nearby orders when you're available
2. You get notification on phone/app
3. Order appears in "Pending Orders"
4. You have 10 minutes to accept/reject

**Accept Order:**
1. Click order
2. Review details (customer address, items, etc.)
3. Click "Accept" if you can deliver
4. You see updated details

**Reject Order:**
1. Click order
2. Click "Reject"
3. Select reason (too far, too late, other)
4. Click "Confirm"
5. Order reassigned to another driver

### View Order Details

Click on any assigned order to see:
- **Customer Name & Phone**: Contact information
- **Delivery Address**: Full address with map
- **Order Items**: What you're delivering
- **Special Instructions**: Any customer notes
- **Estimated Pickup Time**: When to collect package
- **Estimated Delivery Time**: When to deliver by
- **Payment**: Whether already paid or COD
- **Recipient Signature**: If required

## Updating Delivery Status

### Order Status Flow

```
Created (Order placed)
    ↓
Processing (Being prepared)
    ↓
Ready for Pickup (Wait here to collect)
    ↓
Picked Up (You've collected package)
    ↓
In Transit (On your way)
    ↓
Delivered (Received by customer) ✓
    OR
Delivery Failed (Couldn't deliver)
    ↓
Returned (Back to store)
```

### Step 1: Ready for Pickup

When you arrive to collect the package:

1. App shows "Ready for Pickup"
2. You go to store location
3. Scan QR code on order (if available)
4. Or enter order number manually
5. Collect package from staff
6. Verify contents if needed
7. Click "Picked Up"

**Pickup Checklist:**
- Confirm package is correct
- Check for any damage
- Note if anything unusual
- Get signature from store (on app)

### Step 2: In Transit

After pickup:

1. App shows "In Transit"
2. Package loaded in vehicle
3. You start delivery route
4. GPS tracks your location
5. You can add notes: "On my way", "Stuck in traffic", etc.

**In Transit Actions:**
- Update location (manual or automatic)
- Contact customer via app
- Add notes about status
- Upload status photo
- Report any issues

### Step 3: Delivery

When you arrive at customer location:

1. Click "Arriving Soon"
2. App notifies customer
3. Contact customer to confirm they're ready
4. Knock/call customer
5. Verify customer identity
6. Hand over package
7. Get signature (if required):
   - Digital signature on device
   - Photo of customer with package
   - Or verbal confirmation

8. Click "Delivered"
9. Add notes: "Left with John", "Delivery time", etc.
10. Take delivery photo (optional)
11. Submit

**Delivery Checklist:**
- Confirm correct address
- Verify customer identity
- Get required signature
- Note delivery time
- Confirm package condition
- Add any relevant notes

### Step 4: Delivery Failure

If you can't deliver:

1. Click "Delivery Failed"
2. Select reason:
   - Customer not available
   - Wrong address
   - Customer refused
   - Address inaccessible
   - Package damaged
   - Other reason

3. Add detailed notes:
   - What happened
   - When you arrived
   - What customer said
   - Next steps needed

4. Options appear:
   - **Reschedule**: Try again tomorrow
   - **Return to Store**: Send package back
   - **Leave with Someone**: Leave with neighbor
   - **Contact Customer**: Call/message customer

5. Follow system instructions
6. Submit

**Failure Documentation:**
- Be specific about reason
- Include time arrived
- Note exact customer response
- Add photos if applicable (locked door, etc.)

## GPS & Location Tracking

### Enable Location Services

1. Go to Settings in app
2. Click "Location Tracking"
3. Toggle "ON"
4. Allow app permissions:
   - Access to location
   - Background location (for off-screen tracking)
5. Your location now updates every 1-5 minutes

### Benefits of GPS

- **Accuracy**: Precise ETA for customer
- **Safety**: Store knows where you are
- **Route Optimization**: Get best delivery route
- **Customer Service**: Share live location with customer
- **Performance**: Earn bonuses for on-time delivery

### Privacy

- Location data only accessible to store
- Data deleted after 30 days
- You can toggle tracking on/off
- No tracking outside working hours

### View Your Route

1. Click "Show Route" in app
2. See:
   - Your current location
   - Next delivery location
   - Optimal route (by app)
   - Nearby orders
   - Traffic conditions

3. Follow suggested route or choose your own

## Communication

### Contact Customer

Before arrival:

1. In app, click order
2. Click "Contact Customer"
3. Select method:
   - **Call**: Direct phone call
   - **SMS**: Text message
   - **WhatsApp**: WhatsApp message
   - **In-App**: Message through app

4. Example messages:
   - "Hi John! I'm picking up your order, will arrive in 20 mins"
   - "Arriving at your location in 5 minutes"
   - "I'm outside your building, can you come down?"

### Customer Contacts You

If customer calls/messages:

1. In app, click "Messages"
2. You'll see conversation history
3. Respond quickly
4. Common customer requests:
   - Changing delivery address
   - Delaying delivery
   - Needing specific instructions
   - Questions about package

### Add Delivery Notes

After each status update, add notes:

1. Click "Add Note"
2. Write what happened:
   - "Delivered to apartment 5A"
   - "Left with security guard"
   - "Customer requested delivery on Tuesday"
   - "Gate was locked, had to call customer"

3. This helps if customer has questions later
4. Build trust with detailed notes

## Problem Solving

### Customer Not Home

1. Call/WhatsApp customer
2. Ask when they'll be available
3. Options:
   - **Wait**: If customer is 10-15 mins away
   - **Reschedule**: Agree on another time
   - **Return**: Send back to store

4. Update status in app
5. Mark appropriate action

### Wrong Address

1. Contact customer immediately
2. Verify correct address
3. Options:
   - **Go to correct address**: If nearby
   - **Return to store**: If far away
   - **Contact admin**: If confused

4. Update delivery address if customer confirms
5. Report issue to admin

### Package Damaged

1. Take photos of damage
2. Document carefully
3. Contact customer
4. Contact store admin
5. Don't deliver if severely damaged
6. Return to store for investigation

### Customer Refuses Order

1. Ask why they're refusing
2. Call store if needed
3. Document reason clearly
4. Take photo of package with seal
5. Return to store
6. Report refusal to admin

### Lost/Stolen Package

1. Immediately notify admin
2. Provide details:
   - When you last had package
   - Last known location
   - Any witnesses
3. File report
4. Follow admin instructions
5. May be reported to police

## Payment & Earnings

### Delivery Fees

You earn:
- **Per delivery**: Base fee ($X per delivery)
- **Distance bonus**: Extra for far deliveries
- **Performance bonus**: Bonus for on-time deliveries
- **Rating bonus**: Extra if customers rate highly
- **COD commission**: % of collected cash

### Earning Structure

**Example Monthly:**
- 100 deliveries × $2 = $200
- Distance bonus: $30
- On-time bonus (95%): $20
- Ratings (4.8/5): $10
- COD commission: $50
- **Total: $310**

### Payment Method

1. Go to "Earnings" section
2. View:
   - Current month earnings
   - Withdrawals history
   - Withdrawal method

3. Set withdrawal method:
   - Bank transfer
   - Vodafone Cash
   - Cash at office

4. Request withdrawal
5. Payment processed within 2-3 business days

### COD (Cash on Delivery)

If customer pays in cash:

1. Collect payment from customer
2. Verify amount
3. Get signature/receipt
4. In app, click "Cash Received"
5. Enter amount received
6. Submit proof (photo of cash, receipt)
7. Deposit cash at store office
8. Get commission on amount

**Important:**
- Don't give change from personal money
- Ask customer for exact amount
- Always submit proof
- Keep receipts for verification

## Equipment & Safety

### Required Equipment

- **Smartphone**: For app and communication
- **ID**: Always carry identification
- **Vehicle**: Car, motorcycle, or bicycle
- **Safety gear**: Helmet (motorcycle), vest

### Optional Equipment

- **Phone mount**: For GPS navigation
- **Delivery bag**: For package protection
- **Camera**: For documentation
- **Umbrella**: For weather protection

### Safety Tips

1. **Personal Safety:**
   - Avoid dangerous areas at night
   - Trust your instincts
   - Don't carry large amounts of cash
   - Keep emergency contact number
   - Tell someone your route

2. **Package Safety:**
   - Don't leave expensive packages unattended
   - Get signatures for valuable items
   - Leave in safe places if needed
   - Take photos of delivery location
   - Avoid moisture/damage

3. **Vehicle Safety:**
   - Regular maintenance
   - Safe driving practices
   - Proper packaging to avoid shifting
   - Secure load properly
   - Follow traffic rules

## FAQ

**Q: What if I accept order but can't deliver?**
A: Click "Reject" and explain. You won't be penalized if genuine reason. Frequent rejections may affect rating.

**Q: How do I get to pickup location?**
A: Click "Show Route" in app. You'll get directions to store address from store details.

**Q: What if customer won't sign?**
A: Get verbal confirmation and take photo with customer. Note "Refused signature but confirmed receipt".

**Q: Can I deliver multiple orders at once?**
A: Yes! Optimize your route. Deliver nearby orders in sequence. More deliveries = more earnings.

**Q: What if I have extra time between orders?**
A: You can pause availability or accept another order. More orders = more earnings!

**Q: How do I report a problem?**
A: Click "Report Issue" in order details. Select issue type and provide details. Admin will contact you.

**Q: Can I change my working hours?**
A: Yes, in Settings. Update availability anytime. System won't assign orders outside your hours.

**Q: How long do earnings take to process?**
A: Earnings calculated monthly. You can withdraw on 1st of following month.

**Q: What if customer claims wrong item delivered?**
A: Contact admin immediately. Provide photos from delivery. Investigation initiated.

**Q: Can I reject orders from specific areas?**
A: No, but you can set service area in Settings. Won't get orders outside that area.
