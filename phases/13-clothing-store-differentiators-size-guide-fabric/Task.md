# Task 13: Luxury Clothing Store Differentiators (Size Guide, Fabric Details & Stylist Concierge)

## Objective
Implement high-value clothing store features that boost conversion, reduce returns, and enhance the luxury atelier brand experience.

## Scope of Work
1. **Interactive Size Guide & Measurement Drawer**:
   - Comprehensive chart with conversions between US, UK, EU, and Asian sizing.
   - Sizing chart for Tops, Tailoring, and Footwear with bust, chest, waist, hips, and length in cm and inches.
   - "How to Measure" diagrammatic guide.
2. **Fabric & Artisanal Composition Card**:
   - Fabric origin callouts (e.g. "Loro Piana Wool", "Japanese Selvedge Denim", "Mulberry Silk").
   - Fabric weight and seasonal suitability gauge (Lightweight, Midweight, Heavy Winter).
   - Care & Maintenance instructions.
3. **Scarcity & Inventory Urgency Indicators**:
   - "Only 2 Left in Size M" / "Low Stock" indicators for items with low inventory.
   - "Made to Order • Dispatched in 3 Days" for bespoke apparel.
4. **Direct Stylist WhatsApp Concierge**:
   - "Need Styling or Sizing Advice?" button connecting directly to the store's concierge with the specific product name and URL pre-filled.
5. **Quick View Lookbook Modal**:
   - Quick-inspect modal on product cards allowing customers to browse product images, pick color and size, and add to bag without full page reload.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Size guide modal opens smoothly with accurate measurement charts.
- [ ] Fabric composition details display clearly on product cards and product details.
- [ ] Low stock badges alert customers when inventory is below threshold.
- [ ] Stylist WhatsApp button prefills context for personal shopper inquiries.
