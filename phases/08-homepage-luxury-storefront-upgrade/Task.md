# Task 08: Homepage Luxury Storefront Overhaul (`http://localhost:3000/`)

## Objective
Elevate the main storefront homepage into a high-fashion, editorial luxury experience adhering to `ui-ux-pro-max` design intelligence. Integrate an Archival / Past Collections section, refined lookbook curation, interactive filters, and seamless navigation.

## Scope of Work
1. **Hero & Editorial Lookbook**:
   - Monochromatic high-contrast banner with typography pairing (`Bodoni Moda` serif headings + `Jost` sans-serif subheads).
   - Season statement banner (e.g. "Atelier Capsule 2025 • Architectural Tailoring").
2. **Archival / Past Collections Showcase**:
   - Dedicated curated section on the homepage: "The Archive • Past Collections".
   - Cards showcasing iconic past seasons (FW23 Cashmere, SS24 Raw Silk, Monolith Tailoring).
   - Direct navigation to `/collections/past`.
3. **Interactive Category Gateway**:
   - Instant visual navigation cards to **Women's Atelier** (`/women`), **Men's Atelier** (`/men`), **Accessories**, and **Past Collections**.
4. **Enhanced Product Feed**:
   - Integrated with the new interactive `ProductCard` supporting quantity counters, color swatches, dynamic cart button states, and quick-add feedback.
5. **Mobile Responsiveness**:
   - Touch-friendly horizontal swipe for collection lookbooks and sticky bottom navigation on mobile devices.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Homepage renders flawlessly with high contrast `#09090B` and pure `#FFFFFF`.
- [ ] Past Collections section is featured on the homepage with link to archival collections.
- [ ] Gender gateways link directly to `/men` and `/women`.
- [ ] Product feed allows direct color/size selection and quantity counter without page reloads.
