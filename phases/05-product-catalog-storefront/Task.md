# Task 05: Product Catalog & Storefront Experience

## Overview
Elevate the product discovery, lookbook browsing, and customization experience to luxury fashion standards, including zoomable galleries, live inventory counters, multi-currency switcher, and responsive cart drawers.

## Objectives
- [ ] Upgrade product detail page with high-definition image gallery, thumbnail carousel, and zoom-on-hover.
- [ ] Add real-time stock indicator (e.g. *"Only 3 pieces remaining in stock"*).
- [ ] Build sticky "Quick Add" floating bar for seamless mobile shopping.
- [ ] Implement multi-currency switcher (USD, EGP, EUR, AED, SAR, GBP) with automatic formatting.
- [ ] Enhance category lookbooks with editorial layouts, hover previews, and quick-filter pills.

## Dependencies
- Depends on `Task 01: Brand & Design System Core`.
